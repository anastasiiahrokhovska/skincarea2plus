Reading: the story of Rhode

Teacher: Take your time with each step. Let her read silently first. Only help with words after she tries to guess from context. Stages: 1 Before reading (4 min), 2 Skim (3), 3 Read for detail (8), 4 True / False (5), 5 Words in context (4), 6 Numbers (3), 7 Summary (3), 8 Discussion (2).

1. Before you read (4 min)

Do you know the brand Rhode? What do you think it sells? Who started it? Then tap the six key words.

The text

1Hailey Bieber is a model and businesswoman. In June 2022 she started a skincare brand called Rhode. Rhode is her middle name. At first she wanted to call the brand “Bieber Beauty”, but she could not use that name, so she chose a name that was more personal and simple.

2When Rhode began, it had only three products: a face fluid, a cream and a lip treatment. They were the Peptide Glazing Fluid, the Barrier Restore Cream and the Peptide Lip Treatment. The idea was simple: make a small number of things and make each one really good. The products help to keep the skin soft and hydrated, and they protect the skin barrier, the natural shield of your face.

3The brand became famous on Instagram and TikTok. Many people loved the “glazed” look: fresh, soft and shiny skin. The packaging is clean and minimal, and every new product is treated like a big event. Sometimes a product is a limited edition, so people must buy it quickly. Later, Rhode added makeup, like blushes and lip tints. These are hybrid products, because they give colour and also look after your skin.

4In 2025, a company called e.l.f. Beauty acquired Rhode for up to one billion dollars. e.l.f. is known for affordable makeup, so many people said it was a clever match. Hailey did not leave. She stayed with the brand as the founder and creative leader.

5For a long time, you could buy Rhode only online. Then, in September 2025, the brand arrived in Sephora shops in the US and Canada, and in November 2025 in Sephora UK. e.l.f. said the American launch was the biggest brand launch in the history of Sephora. In just three years, a small brand with three products became a global beauty name.
